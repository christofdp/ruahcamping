Ons storieOur story

Kamp tussen die wingerdeCamp among the vineyards

Soos vir so baie ander het dinge in die grendeltyd van 2020 deurmekaar geloop. Kinders kon nie skool toe nie, en ons het baie tyd in die berg deurgebring om uit die huis te kom.Like so many others, life was turned upside down during the 2020 lockdown. The children couldn't go to school, and we spent a lot of time in the mountains to get out of the house.

So het ons begin kamp bou: eers 'n gelyk staanplek vir die wa, toe 'n braaiplek en lopende water, later krag en badkamers. Gou wou ander ook kom. Ons het soveel stilte en rus hier gevind, 'n verfrissing van ons gees. Daarom die naam Ruah: Gees, asem (Ps 104:30, Job 32:8). Ons deel dit graag met ander.So we started building a camp: first a level stand for the caravan, then a braai and running water, later power and bathrooms. Soon others wanted to come too. We found so much quiet and rest here, a refreshing of the spirit. Hence the name Ruah: Spirit, breath (Ps 104:30, Job 32:8). We are glad to share it.

Die kamp lê op 'n werkende tafeldruifplaas langs die Elskloof-rivier. Dit is ideaal vir tente, kampeerwaens en bossleepwaens, en 'n voertuig met hoër grondvryhoogte.The camp is on a working table grape farm beside the Elskloof river. It suits tents, camping trailers and off-road caravans, and a vehicle with higher ground clearance.

GeskiedenisHistory

Krag uit die kloofPower from the kloof

In 1902 het die Dicey-familie van die Cape Orchard Company die moontlikheid van hidro-elektrisiteit in die Elskloof ondersoek. Die Pelton-wiel het per boot uit Italië gekom en is van Kaapstad se hawe met perdewaens na Orchard vervoer.In 1902 the Dicey family of the Cape Orchard Company explored hydro-electric power in the Elskloof. The Pelton wheel came by ship from Italy and was carried from Cape Town harbour to Orchard by horse wagon.

Die krag het die koelkamers by Orchard-stasie, 'n konfytfabriek, die pakstoor en die omliggende huise voorsien, 42 jaar lank tot die vallei in 1944 by Worcester se steenkoolkragsentrale aangesluit is. Die murasies van die sentrale is net bokant die kamp, en langs die paadjie in die kloof sien jy nog die ou ysters, nissies en pype.It powered the cold stores at Orchard station, a jam factory, the packing shed and nearby homes for 42 years, until the valley was linked to Worcester's coal power station in 1944. The ruins of the station are just above the camp, and along the kloof path you can still see the old irons, niches and pipes.

NatuurleweWildlife

Fynbos aan die skadukantFynbos on the shady side

Hier aan die skadukant van die Hexrivierberge groei groter bome en struike as deel van die Kaapse fynbos: die protea-familie, sandolyf, kliphout en hoër op in die kloof ook waterbome.On this shady side of the Hex River mountains, bigger trees and shrubs grow as part of the Cape fynbos: the protea family, sand olive, kliphout and, higher up the kloof, water trees.

KampreëlsCamp rules

Sodat almal dit kan genietSo everyone can enjoy it

  1. Geen harde of versterkte musiek nie.No loud or amplified music.
  2. Stilte tussen 22:00 en 08:00.Quiet between 10 pm and 8 am.
  3. Geen swem by die keerwal of in die opgaardam nie: dit voorsien drinkwater aan sowat 90 gesinne. Swem gerus by die rotspoel.No swimming at the weir or in the reservoir: it supplies drinking water to about 90 families. Swim at the rock pool instead.
  4. Los net jou voetspore. Vullisdromme is beskikbaar.Leave only your footprints. Rubbish bins are available.
  5. Hou die badkamers asseblief netjies, soos jy dit graag sou wou kry.Please leave the bathrooms as neat as you'd like to find them.
  6. Geen vure buite die geboude braaiplekke nie.No fires outside the built braais.
  7. Beskerm die natuur, die San-rotskuns en ander erfenis.Protect nature, the San rock art and other heritage.
  8. Geen gewere, jag of teikenskiet nie.No guns, hunting or target shooting.
  9. Plaaspersoneel bestuur soms die besproeiing by die opgaardam. Hulle doen net hul werk en sal jou nie pla nie.Farm staff sometimes manage irrigation at the reservoir. They're just doing their job and won't bother you.
  10. Stap, draf en ry gerus fiets op die plaas.Feel free to walk, jog and cycle around the farm.
